Since Years

A Legacy of Taste

Foodway Family Restaurant was born from a simple dream — to serve food that feels like home. Located in the heart of Bhandup West on L.B.S. Marg, we have spent years perfecting the art of authentic North Indian, Mughlai, and Chinese cuisine.

What started as a small eatery has grown into Bhandup's most beloved family restaurant, where every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and culinary excellence. Our name, 'The King of Taste,' isn't just a tagline — it's a promise we deliver with every plate.

We believe that dining is more than just eating. It's about creating memories, sharing laughter, and savoring moments with the people who matter most.
