Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, we recommend reservations for groups of 5 or more, especially on weekends and holidays, to ensure we have the best table ready for you.

What cuisines do you serve?

We serve North Indian, Mughlai, Punjabi, Chinese, and Seafood dishes. Our menu features 200+ items ranging from tandoori specialities and rich Mughlai gravies to Indo-Chinese favourites.

Do you serve beef?

No, we do not serve beef. All our meat preparations are Halal certified. We take pride in respecting local dietary preferences while serving the finest quality ingredients.

Is the restaurant family-friendly?

Absolutely! We are a family restaurant at heart. Our spacious seating, welcoming atmosphere, and varied menu with kid-friendly options make us perfect for family outings of all sizes.

What are your seating options?

We offer two unique dining experiences: our air-conditioned dining halls for a comfortable modern experience, and our traditional Dastarkhan floor seating for an authentic royal Mughlai ambiance. Both setups accommodate groups of 10–20 people.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes! We deliver through Zomato and Swiggy. You can also order for takeaway by calling us at +91 74002 21190. Enjoy our food from the comfort of your home.

What is the average cost for two?

A meal for two at Foodway typically costs approximately ₹800 to ₹1,000, making us one of the most affordable family dining options in Bhandup with generous portions and authentic taste.

Do you host private events?

Yes, we accommodate birthday parties, anniversary celebrations, corporate gatherings, and family reunions. Contact us to discuss your event requirements and we'll create a customised dining experience for your occasion.

Is parking available?

Street parking is available near the restaurant on L.B.S. Marg. We are also easily accessible from Nahur Railway Station, which is just 0.54 km away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and digital wallets for your convenience.
